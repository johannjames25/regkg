# Large Exposures Reporting Directive (illustrative)

Issued by the Financial Supervisory Authority. This text is fictional and written for prototyping only.

§1 Scope and frequency
1.1 This Directive applies to all scheduled commercial banks.
1.2 Banks shall report large exposures on a quarterly basis.

§2 Definitions
2.1 "Exposure" means all on-balance sheet assets and off-balance sheet items. Off-balance sheet items shall be converted into credit equivalent amounts using the credit conversion factors specified under the Standardised Approach for credit risk.
2.2 "Group of connected counterparties" means two or more natural or legal persons where one of them has, directly or indirectly, control over the other or others; or between whom there is economic interdependence such that financial problems of one would likely result in repayment difficulties for the others.
2.3 "Eligible capital base" means the Tier 1 capital of the bank as reported in its most recent capital adequacy return.

§3 Reporting obligation
3.1 A bank shall report every exposure to a counterparty or a group of connected counterparties whose value is equal to or above 10 percent of its eligible capital base.
3.2 In addition, a bank shall report its 20 largest exposures, irrespective of the threshold in 3.1.

§4 Exemptions
4.1 Exposures to the central government and the central bank are exempt from large exposure limits but shall nevertheless be reported.
4.2 Intraday interbank exposures shall be excluded from the report.

§5 Credit risk mitigation
5.1 The exposure value may be reduced by eligible financial collateral, namely cash and government securities, after applying the haircuts specified under the Standardised Approach.

§6 Submission
6.1 The report shall be submitted within 15 calendar days from the end of the quarter through the Authority's reporting portal in the prescribed format LE-1.

§7 Data governance
7.1 Banks shall maintain documented data lineage for all reported figures.
7.2 Reported exposures shall be reconciled with the general ledger.
